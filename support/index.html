Orgo Support

For help with your library, a purchase or a backup, email rmbrown323@icloud.com. Include your device, OS version, Orgo version and what happened.

Photo access and cleanup

Review the permission under device Settings → Apps → Orgo → Photos. With limited access, Orgo can work only with the photos you selected.

Remove marks a photo for review. Open Review to check your choices. When you confirm deletion, Apple asks for permission. Cancelling that prompt leaves the photo in your library.

Orgo 1.1.2 · Automatic Backup

Open Orgo Settings → iCloud Backup to manage Automatic Backup. If Orgo reports that both copies changed, Restore iCloud Backup uses the cloud copy. Replace iCloud Backup uses the copy on this device. Check which copy you want to keep before confirming.

Orgo 1.1.3 · iCloud Sync

From Library, open Settings → iCloud Sync. Use the same Apple Account and iCloud Photos library on both devices. Open Orgo on each device to allow changes to sync. Turning sync off leaves your local progress in place.

The old Automatic Backup does not migrate to the new sync system. An older manual backup that lacks matching photo identifiers may not restore in 1.1.3. If you depend on an older backup, keep the original file. The new version can export a fresh backup of your current progress.

Orgo 1.1.3 · Manual Backup and reset

Open Settings → Manual Backup to export, share or restore review progress and supported settings. Backups contain no photos. Import replaces this device’s progress after confirmation and turns iCloud Sync off. A local recovery copy is saved before import.

Settings → Reset All App Data clears local review progress, settings and app-created recovery copies. Your Apple Photos library, exported backup files and iCloud data stay in their respective locations. The daily review allowance and reward records survive reset.

Orgo Unlimited

Use Restore Purchases on the Unlimited screen with the Apple Account used for the original purchase. Apple handles billing and subscription management. You can manage a subscription from your Apple Account settings.

Contacting support

Include a screenshot only if you are comfortable sharing what it shows. In version 1.1.3, the in-app Support and Send Feedback options prepare an editable email with the app and device details. You choose whether to send it.
