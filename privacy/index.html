Privacy Policy

Orgo processes photos on your device. There are no ads, developer analytics or tracking. Here is what stays on your device and what happens when you turn on an optional feature.

Photos and local progress

Orgo uses the Photos access you grant to show photos and apply your choices. Marking Remove stages a choice. Deleting a photo requires confirmation in Apple’s system prompt. You can change Photos access in your device settings.

Review choices, preferences, daily allowances and progress are stored on your device. If you allow reminders, iOS schedules them locally.

Orgo 1.1.2 · iCloud Backup

Automatic Backup is optional. If you turn it on, Orgo stores a copy of your review data and settings in your private iCloud Drive container. Backups contain no photos. You can turn Automatic Backup off or choose Delete iCloud Data in Orgo’s iCloud Backup settings. The developer cannot read your private iCloud data.

Orgo 1.1.3 · iCloud Sync and manual files

In version 1.1.3, iCloud Sync is optional. If you turn it on, Orgo stores review choices, progress, photo identifiers and supported settings in your private CloudKit database under your Apple Account. Your photos stay in Apple Photos. The developer cannot read this private database. Switching sync off retains both your device data and the existing cloud data. Manage cloud data through your device’s iCloud storage settings.

Manual Backup lets you export or share a file containing review data and supported settings. You choose its destination and can delete exported files there. When you import, Orgo saves a recovery copy on your device before replacing local progress and settings. Reset All App Data clears local review data, recovery copies, settings and the location cache. Daily allowance and reward records survive reset. Exported files and private iCloud data are managed separately.

Optional photo tools

Discreet Mode runs its checks on your device. If you enable Location in Photo Info in version 1.1.3, Orgo sends saved photo coordinates to Apple Maps to obtain a place name. It does not send the photo or request your current location. Results are cached locally for up to 30 days. Turn Location off to stop new lookups.

Purchases, sharing and contact

Apple handles Unlimited purchases. Orgo checks your purchase status. The developer does not receive your payment card details. When you share a photo or backup, the destination you choose receives it under its own privacy practices.

If you send a support email, the developer receives your email address, message and any included diagnostics or attachments. You can review the email before sending it. Contact rmbrown323@icloud.com for privacy questions or to request deletion of support correspondence.
